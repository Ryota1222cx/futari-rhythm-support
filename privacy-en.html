Privacy Policy

Futari Rhythm ("the App") is provided by Ryota Masuda ("the Operator") and diagnoses compatibility between two people from a LINE chat export. This policy explains what the App handles, where it goes and how to delete it.

1. Chat history

The chat file you choose is read and analysed only on your device. The full text, both names, word lists and results are never sent to the Operator or any outside service. The Operator runs no servers.

A chat export also contains the other person's messages and name. The App never takes them off your device. Showing names or words on a share card is off by default; check with the other person before sharing a card that shows them.

2. Kept on your device

  • The score, type and numbers such as reply times
  • Top 20 words, up to 10 short phrases sent twice or more, words that rose or fell between the first and second half (5 each)
  • Both display names
  • Records of opened detailed diagnoses (purchase transaction IDs)

The copy of the chosen file is deleted after reading. This information is excluded from iCloud and Google backups, and is removed with "Delete all results" in Settings or by deleting the App.

3. Sent outside the App

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): To verify purchases, an anonymous identifier created by RevenueCat and purchase records (product, transaction ID, time). No chat content or names.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, United States): When the App stops unexpectedly, the error type, where in the code it happened, and the device model and OS version, so the problem can be fixed. No error text, interaction history, chat content or names.
  • Apple and Google: Purchases go through the App Store and Google Play.

The App shows no ads and does no tracking for advertising.

4. Purposes

Diagnosing compatibility, verifying purchases and fixing bugs. Nothing else.

5. Deletion

Data on your device can be deleted as described in section 2. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are linked to your Apple or Google purchases and cannot be deleted from the App; email us if you want them deleted.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes will be posted on this page. If the App starts handling new information or sending it to new parties, we will also tell you in the App.
