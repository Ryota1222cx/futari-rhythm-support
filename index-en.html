Futari Rhythm Support

Futari Rhythm is an iPhone and Android app that reads a two-person LINE chat export and counts reply speed, who starts conversations and frequently used words to give a compatibility score and type. Reading and analysis happen only on your device, and the chat content is never sent anywhere. No account is needed. The app is in Japanese.

The result is for entertainment. It is calculated only from message times and word counts and does not judge feelings or relationships.

Contact

For bugs, feedback or purchases, email m.ryota1222@gmail.com. Please do not send chat files or screenshots of chats.

FAQ

  • Exporting a chat: In LINE, open the chat, open Settings from the menu at the top right and choose "Export chat history". Save it to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android), then choose it in the app.
  • Trying it first: "Try with a sample chat" walks through a diagnosis using a fictional two-person chat.
  • Is my chat saved?: The full text is never saved. The app keeps the numbers it calculated, top 20 words, up to 10 short phrases sent twice or more, words that rose or fell (5 each) and the two display names. The copy of the chosen file is deleted after reading.
  • Detailed diagnosis: A one-time purchase per person (not a subscription). Each purchase opens one diagnosis.
  • Deleting data: In the app, open Settings and tap "Delete all results". Results are not included in device backups, so they are lost when the app is deleted or the phone is replaced.
